1. Reserve reviewer

The form asks for a senior author holding a PhD or equivalent seniority to serve as reserve reviewer. This paper is single-authored work carried out as an undergraduate student, so no author meets that requirement. In the required field I entered [WHAT YOU ENTERED IN THE RESERVE REVIEWER FIELD]. I did not want to add a co-author who had not made a substantial intellectual contribution, as that would conflict with the ACM authorship policy. Please let me know how you would like this handled.

2. Self-citation disclosure

One of the seven KV-cache compression methods evaluated in the paper (DKV) is a publicly released method that I authored. For double-blind review it is cited without its author line or archive DOI, and the full reference will be restored in the camera-ready. The method name still appears in the paper's figures and tables, so the de-identification is partial; renaming it would have meant altering how the measured data is presented.

3. Previous submissions

The change note states that the prior TMLR submission identifiers are available on request. They are 11745 and 12029. Both were desk-rejected without external review, and no reviewer reports were produced.

4. Format-checker warnings

The submission checker reported two warnings that I believe are false positives:

- Margins: text height exceeding 9in by up to 4% on odd-numbered pages. The text block is 646pt (8.94in) and begins 1.00in from the top of the page. The excess matches acmart's header box (headheight 13pt + headsep 14pt = 27pt), and a stock acmart file with the same class options measures the same.

- Body font: 9.3pt text on pages 7-8. Body text is 10pt throughout; the flagged text is two tables set at 9pt, which the CFP permits for tables. When I set them at 10pt, LaTeX could no longer place two of the four tables.

I am happy to make any change you would prefer on any of these points.
